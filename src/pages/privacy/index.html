Sendlyr privacy policy.

Clear boundaries for website data handling.

Website analytics

Analytics is disabled unless production safeguards and privacy review are complete.

When enabled, events use random identifiers. They exclude names, emails, and message content.

Booking attribution

Booking links may include a random attribution identifier.

Sendlyr may match that identifier with Calendly booking data on the server.

Contact data

Information submitted directly is used to respond to your request.

Server credentials never appear in the browser.

Retention and deletion

Raw attribution events are designed for short retention. Aggregates contain no direct identifiers.

Request access or deletion at privacy@sendlyr.com.

Processors

Sendlyr may use Vercel, Supabase, and Calendly for hosting, storage, and scheduling.

Each processor applies its own security and privacy terms.
