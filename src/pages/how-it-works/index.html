Five working steps

A short path to evidence.

Each step closes a specific decision gap.

  1. Frame the retention question.

    Choose the outcome and observation window.

  2. Map candidate behaviors.

    Trace the journey before the retained state.

  3. Rank the evidence.

    Compare prevalence, timing, and retention separation.

  4. Design the intervention.

    Define treatment, control, exposure, and success.

  5. Write the operating rule.

    State who receives what action, and when.

Evidence guardrails

Every claim keeps its boundary.

Correlation

Ranks a behavior. It does not prove an intervention caused the outcome.

Leading indicator

Shows movement toward activation. It may precede verified retention.

Controlled lift

Compares treatment and control. See the Typesy method notes.

Operating rule

Changes only when new evidence justifies the change.
