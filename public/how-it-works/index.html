How it works

Five steps from your product data
to emails that actually move users.

No new stack. No generic sequences. Human approval before anything sends.

01
Connect your data
We read from your existing behavioral data source — product database, analytics platform, or event stream. No new SDK to install, no code changes required.
Currently live: Azure SQL. Expanding to other sources.
→
02
Discover your activation milestone
We run a PAI analysis — a statistical scan of your historical cohort data to find the one early behavior that predicts whether a user converts or stays. This is analysis, not a formula: the behavior is different for every product.
Example: "Complete ≥1 course within 4 days" → 39.5% retention vs. 34% baseline in Typesy.
→
03
Classify every user, every day
Each trial user is assigned a behavioral state daily based on what they have or haven't done relative to the activation milestone. States are deterministic — no ML, no black box.
Typesy VIP runs 9 states: NEW, IN_PROGRESS, STUCK, ACTIVATED, and five more.
→
04
Generate emails, you approve them
For each state, we generate a behavior-matched email draft you can review, edit, and approve once. After that, the system personalizes each send — names, progress stats, dynamic CTAs — from live data.
One approval per state template. No per-user approval. You can edit or replace any draft before approving.
→
05
Measure the experiment
Sendlyr runs a treatment/control A/B split from day one — state-matched emails vs. nothing. Conversion rate, PAI hit rate, and send performance are reported to you weekly.
Minimum signal threshold: 50 treatment users. Results update daily in your dashboard (client-specific access).
Common questions
Do I need to change my stack or install an SDK?
No. We read from your existing data source. The only integration is read access to your behavioral data.
How long does setup take?
48-hour analysis to identify the activation milestone. 1–2 weeks from first call to first email in monitoring mode — emails are generated and verified but nothing sends until you approve.
Who approves the emails?
You do — once per state template. After that, the system personalizes and sends at the right time with no further approval required per user.
