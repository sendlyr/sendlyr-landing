Five working steps

From product data to a sharper indicator.

Each step keeps the behavior, lifecycle guidance, and measured outcome connected.

  1. Bring product data in.

    Connect the events, eligible cohort, and outcome from the systems you already use.

  2. Find the leading indicator.

    Identify the early behavior, time window, and coverage most useful for lifecycle guidance.

  3. Guide users toward it.

    Turn the indicator into a clear direction for your existing lifecycle stack.

  4. Return measured outcomes.

    Bring the behavior and business result back into Sendlyr with the uncertainty visible.

  5. Improve the indicator.

    Use the new evidence to sharpen the behavior, window, and coverage.

Evidence guardrails

Every claim keeps its boundary.

Correlation

Shows an association. It does not prove the behavior caused the outcome.

Leading indicator

Identifies an early behavior connected to the defined outcome. It guides the lifecycle stack; it is not the outcome itself.

Measured outcome

Shows what changed after the lifecycle guidance, with the uncertainty stated. See the corrected Typesy case study, where the behavior moved and conversion did not.

Operating rule

Earns a go, stop, or iterate conclusion only from the complete readout.
